Privacy Terms

Know what a video says before you watch it.

A Summarise button on every YouTube card. Click it and the summary opens underneath, with timestamps you can jump to. No tab switching, no pasting links anywhere.

How to install

Free. Works on your home feed, subscriptions, the watch page and the sidebar.
Not on the Chrome Web Store yet — it is in private testing with a handful of people first.

Why it's free

It runs on a cheap model and a weekly allowance, which is enough to give away. Most summaries appear straight away.

If a summary is wrong, give it a thumbs down. Once enough people agree it's bad, it gets rewritten.

What you get

Free 400 minutes of video a week. Re-opening something you've already summarised is free and doesn't count.
Cost Nothing. There's no payment method to enter.

Installing

  1. Download the .zip you were sent and unzip it somewhere you will not delete by accident.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), then click Load unpacked and pick that folder.
  3. Sign in with Google. This is what your weekly allowance is attached to.
  4. Go to YouTube. Hover any video and hit Summarise.

Once the Web Store listing is live this becomes a single click and the steps above go away.

What it does not do

It doesn't track what you watch. The only videos it ever knows about are the ones where you press Summarise yourself. There are no ads, no trackers, and nothing is sold to anyone — see the privacy policy, which is short.
